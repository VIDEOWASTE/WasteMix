WasteMix

Live 4-channel video mixer for iPad. Coming soon to the App Store.

Mix cameras, capture cards, video files, images, and NDI® network sources in real time with a GPU-accelerated engine. Built for VJs and live visual artists.

  • Mixer — four channels, 33 blend modes, A/B crossfader, and mix, cut, dip and 10 wipe transitions.
  • Effects — 20 real-time GPU effects, freeze frame, luma and chroma key, picture-in-picture, color correction.
  • Modulation — LFOs, tap-tempo BPM sync, and audio-reactive visuals with a 7-band EQ.
  • Sources — iPad cameras, USB/HDMI capture cards, NDI® sources, videos and images from Photos, 15 audio visualizers and test patterns.
  • Output — NDI® output, external displays over HDMI/USB-C, multi-screen projection mapping with mesh warp, corner pin and edge blending, and recording to Photos.

Requires an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. No account, no tracking, no ads.
