Requirements

An iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. NDI® needs the iPad and the other devices on the same local network. External displays and capture cards connect over USB-C (or an adapter).

Getting started

  1. Tap SOURCE on a channel and pick a camera, a video or image from Photos, an NDI® source, a test pattern, or an audio visualizer.
  2. On an empty mixer, the first source's fader rises to full automatically, so it appears in the PGM (program) monitor right away. Later sources start at zero so nothing jumps on screen mid-show.
  3. Raise faders to mix channels together. Use the blend mode menu (NORM), FX and PARAMS for effects, and COLOR for color correction.
  4. Use the crossfader and the A/B buttons to fade between groups of channels, or GO to run a channel's transition.
  5. Open ADVANCED OUTPUT to send the program to a projector or external display, map it onto slices, and set up NDI output.

Common questions

NDI sources don't show up

Make sure your iPad and the NDI sender are on the same network, and that WasteMix has Local Network access: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → WasteMix. Some guest or venue Wi-Fi networks block device discovery; a dedicated router works best.

Other apps can't see WasteMix's NDI output

WasteMix sends its program output over NDI automatically as “WasteMix Program”. If it's missing, open ADVANCED OUTPUT and check that Program NDI is on under NDI OUTPUT. The feed is 640 × 480 at 30 fps so it stays reliable over Wi-Fi. Individual screens can also be sent as their own NDI outputs by setting their destination to NDI.

Camera or microphone isn't working

WasteMix asks for the camera the first time you pick a camera source, and for the microphone the first time you open AUDIO. If you tapped Don't Allow, turn them back on in Settings → WasteMix. The microphone is only used for audio-reactive visuals and is never recorded.

Capture cards and USB cameras

Plug a UVC-compatible HDMI capture card or USB webcam into the iPad's USB-C port. It appears under SOURCE → External Capture (UVC) and updates as devices are plugged in or removed.

External displays and projection mapping

Connect a display or projector over USB-C or an HDMI adapter. In ADVANCED OUTPUT, choose Display as the screen's destination. From there you can add slices, move and resize them, warp them with a mesh grid or corner pin, and soften edges where projectors overlap.

Recording

Tap REC to record the program output. Finished recordings appear in MEDIA, ready to load as a source, and are also saved to Photos if you allow it.

Presets

SAVE stores the whole mixer state, including each channel's settings. LOAD brings it back.

Credits and version

Tap the ⓘ button in the top-left corner of the mixer for the About screen, including NDI® credits.
