# WasteMix — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 3, 2026_

WasteMix ("the app") is a real-time video mixing application for live visual
performance. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what the app does and
does not do with your data. In short: **WasteMix does not collect, store, or
transmit any personal data to us or to any third party.**

## Data We Collect

**None.** WasteMix has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.

## Device Features the App Uses

WasteMix uses the following device capabilities **locally on your device**, only
while you are using the corresponding feature. This content is processed in real
time for video mixing and is **never uploaded to us or any third party**:

- **Camera** — used as a live video source for mixing. Camera frames are
  rendered on-device and are not recorded or transmitted unless you explicitly
  choose to save output to your photo library.
- **Microphone** — used only for audio-reactive visual effects that respond to
  sound in real time. Audio is analyzed on-device and is not recorded or stored.
- **Photo Library** — used to import your videos and images as mixing sources,
  and to save program-output recordings back to your library when you choose to.
  Access happens only through the standard iOS/iPadOS system pickers.
- **Local Network** — used to discover and exchange NDI® video sources with
  other devices on your local network. This traffic stays on your local network
  and is not routed to us. No local-network data is collected or stored by us.

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

## Children's Privacy

WasteMix does not collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.
